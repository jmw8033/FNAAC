    Controls & rules

    W/S drive. A/D rotate the hull. Aim with the mouse and hold click to fire. Space brakes.

    Each hit deals 34 damage. Respawn after three seconds. The gold ring protects you for two seconds; firing ends that protection.

    Session scores reset when you leave. Lifetime stats are saved. Inactive players leave after three minutes. Switching tabs does not pause a live match.
