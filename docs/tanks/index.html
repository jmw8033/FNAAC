Field guide & controls

Every tank goes down in one hit. You can have five shells and two mines active. Normal shells bounce once; shells cancel each other. Mines explode after ten seconds, or sooner when shot or approached after arming. Blasts pass through walls and destroy cracked blocks. Shells fly across dark pits; tanks cannot.

Brown sentries stay put. Ash tanks patrol. Marine tanks fire fast rockets. Yellow tanks lay mines. Pink tanks fire bursts. Green snipers bank fast shots twice. Violet tanks pursue you. White tanks turn invisible—follow their tracks. Black tanks combine speed, rockets and mines.

Solo: three lives, an extra life every five cleared missions. Defeated enemies stay defeated when you retry. Finish 20 missions to unlock a 100-mission run. Your best result and unlock are saved on this browser.

Two players: player 1 uses the normal controls. Player 2 uses arrow keys to move, I/J/K/L to aim, Enter to fire, and right Shift to mine. Alternatively use a gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger fires, A lays mines. First pad controls player 2 in duo, player 1 in solo. Friendly fire is on. A fallen teammate returns in the next mission; losing both ends the run.

Reinforcements adds three enemies. Azure bulwarks absorb shells in their forward shield; attack from the sides or use mines. Coral scatterguns fire a three-shot fan and retreat when approached. Crimson lancers flash before a short, locked-direction dash, then pause to recover. They cannot fire during the dash and do not deal collision damage.

Three original songs change layers with the strongest enemy still alive. Killing advanced enemies removes the intense layers; Brown and Ash enemies leave only the peaceful arrangement. Music can be turned off or adjusted separately from effects.

Use the Mission Workshop to design, reorder, import, export and test campaigns. Select Reinforcements to meet the new tanks. Custom campaigns do not change your classic unlocks.
