Controls & rules

One hit destroys a tank. Five active shells; two mines. Standard shells bounce once. Shells cancel each other. Mines destroy cracked walls; shots cross pits.

Solo: three lives, one extra every five missions. Clear Classic mission 20 to unlock 100 missions.

Player 2: arrows move, I/J/K/L aim, Enter fires, right Shift lays mines. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger fires, A lays mines. First pad controls P2 in duo, P1 in solo.
